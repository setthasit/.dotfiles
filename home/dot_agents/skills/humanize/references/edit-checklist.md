# Edit Checklist

Reached from `SKILL.md` in Edit or Detect mode, before returning. Answer each line pass or fail. A fail → fix the draft and run the list again.

## Detect mode

- Each hit names the pattern, quotes the line, and gives a short fix.
- No rewrite, no score, no claim about whether AI wrote it.

## Meaning and voice

1. The user's point survives with no added claim, example, number, quote, or opinion.
2. The writer's vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, and digressions survive.
3. Strong sentences are untouched. No paragraph was rewritten only to match the others.
4. The cut is proportional to the actual slop. Nothing with character was compressed away.
5. Setup that adds context or tension stays. Generic setup is gone.
6. The structure is the writer's unless it hurt the piece, and any reorder is listed.

## Sentences and words

7. Each generic sentence passes the portability test or was cut.
8. Active voice with real subjects, and direct verbs, where the draft supports them.
9. Tangled sentences are split. Clear spoken cadence stays.
10. No word from the cut lists remains outside a quote.
11. No em dash. No semicolon inside a sentence.

## Patterns

12. Every row of the pattern table in `SKILL.md` is gone or was flagged for the user.
13. Weasel attribution has a named source, or the user was asked for one.
14. The piece ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action, not a kicker or a recap.
15. No formatting slop: no emoji heading, decorative bold, two-item list, or header over a tiny section.

## Final read

16. The writer would recognize the draft as their own voice.
17. It sounds natural read aloud to a sharp colleague.
18. The output holds the full edited draft and a short **What changed** list.
